## 2. Problem

### Section headline
**Having many subscriptions isn't the problem. Having them scattered is.**

### Problem 1 — You don't know the total
**66.1%** of Korean consumers run 3 or more subscriptions; **47%** of our respondents run 4 or more.
Yet **48%** say they can't tell what they spend on subscriptions each month.
Different billing dates mean the total only shows up on the card statement — after the fact.

### Problem 2 — You miss the billing date
Only **15%** know their exact payment, renewal, and benefit-expiry dates.
The other **85%** know some of them, or none.
**56%** have had a free trial roll into a paid charge, and **49%** say they were never warned properly.

### Problem 3 — You pay twice for benefits you already have
**82%** have discovered a benefit they'd been missing only after signing up.
The top reason people don't use a membership they own: **"I didn't know how"** (**42%**).
That's how an OTT service already bundled with your telecom plan ends up billed separately too.

### Problem 4 (supporting) — Managing it means typing it all in
**59%** never put their subscription dates in a calendar.
The number-one reason: **"too tedious to enter manually" (54.2%)**.
People aren't unwilling to manage. Managing just costs too much effort.

## 3. Services

### Section headline
**Check → decide → save. Three features for that exact sequence.**

### Service 1 — Subscription dashboard
Total subscriptions, this month's expected charges, what's billing next, and what needs a decision — all on the first screen.
No app-hopping to piece it together.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target outcome | Full picture in **one screen** instead of several apps |
| Design threshold | **90%+** task success in usability testing |
| Problem it solves | 68% search across multiple apps; 83% call that tedious |

### Service 2 — Subscription calendar + advance alerts
Payment dates, renewals, free-trial endings, and benefit expiries in one month view — with an alert **3 days before** each charge.
Sits alongside personal events, with a filter to show subscriptions only.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target outcome | **Zero** unwanted auto-charges, by prompting a check before billing |
| Design threshold | **60%+** pre-payment review rate (baseline reset after beta) |
| Problem it solves | Only 15% know their key dates; 69% want advance alerts |

### Service 3 — Benefit check
Your benefits sorted by state: held, usable now, expiring soon.
Overlaps with what you already pay for get flagged, and using a benefit takes you straight to the official service.
No automatic cancellations or plan changes. You make the call.

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target outcome | Clearing one duplicate saves **₩7,900–17,000 per month** |
| Design threshold | **30%+** hand-off rate to the official service |
| Problem it solves | 82% learn of benefits late; 65% rate their awareness low |

### Service 4 (supporting) — Register from a screenshot
Upload a screenshot of your card app's payment history and AI drafts the service name, amount, billing cycle, and next payment date.
Every field is editable, and **nothing enters your list until you confirm it.**

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target outcome | **Under 30 seconds** to add one subscription |
| Problem it solves | 54.2% stop managing because manual entry is tedious |
